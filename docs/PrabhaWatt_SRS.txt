SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS)
=========================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Build a multi-tenant web decision-support platform for (a) individual residential consumers, (b) RWAs/eligible residential communities, and (c) MSMEs. It shall screen regulatory pathways, estimate solar economics, optimise affordability-constrained collective projects, simulate and reconcile allocations, manage quotes and approvals, and track maintenance. Shared credit arrangements must be gated by a verified applicable mechanism.

2. STAKEHOLDERS AND ROLES
R-01 Resident/participant: edit own bill, budget, consent and proposals; view approved aggregate summaries.
R-02 Coordinator/RWA: manage invitations, project inputs, proposed policies, approvals, published reports.
R-03 MSME owner: assess own consumption, CAPEX/RESCO options, quotations, performance.
R-04 Installer/financier: submit scoped quotes/terms with no default access to participant private data.
R-05 Technician: receive assigned maintenance work orders and scoped asset details.
R-06 Auditor/admin: inspect versioned calculations, evidence and audit events with justified access.

3. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (MUST/SHOULD/LATER)
FR-001 MUST: Sign-in, role-based authorisation and organisation/project tenancy.
FR-002 MUST: Project creation with geography, DISCOM, consumer class, connection/metering arrangement, roof rights.
FR-003 MUST: Explicit consent before uploading or sharing electricity bills and participant budgets.
FR-004 MUST: Manual/CSV bill ingestion with period, units, billed amount, tariff category, source, verification status.
FR-005 MUST: Validate duplicate periods, nonpositive kWh, missing dates, mismatched periods and invalid amounts.
FR-006 MUST: Policy registry with source URL/document, effective dates, jurisdiction, consumer classes and permitted models.
FR-007 MUST: Eligibility outcome ELIGIBLE_AS_MODELLED / REQUIRES_APPROVAL / INELIGIBLE / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; reasons shown.
FR-008 MUST: Block unsupported mixed household/MSME shared-credit assumptions and automatic subsidy claims.
FR-009 MUST: Feasibility calculation using roof area, capacity, yield, degradation, O&M, tariff and installer quote assumptions.
FR-010 MUST: Compare baseline grid costs and eligible solar CAPEX/RESCO financing scenarios.
FR-011 MUST: Community invitation, participant consent, monthly contribution ceiling and investment pledge.
FR-012 MUST: Constrained optimisation maximising eligible affordable participants, with project-funding, roof, capacity, budget, eligibility and allocation constraints.
FR-013 MUST: Return solver status (optimal/feasible/infeasible/timeout), explanation of exclusions and assumptions.
FR-014 MUST: Model ownership-proportional official-credit shares only when permitted and approved; consumption/hybrid as internal comparison unless expressly authorised.
FR-015 MUST: Distinguish generation kWh, export kWh, financial benefit INR, internal costs and official DISCOM settlement.
FR-016 MUST: Reconcile shares to 100%, cost totals, benefit totals, rounding and prevent double-credit deduction.
FR-017 MUST: Approval lifecycle: DRAFT -> MEMBER_APPROVED_PROPOSAL -> DISCOM_SUBMITTED -> DISCOM_APPROVED -> OFFICIAL_SETTLEMENT_IMPORTED -> RECONCILED; no skipped proof steps.
FR-018 MUST: Printable/exportable scenario report and INTERNAL allocation statement prominently marked NOT A UTILITY BILL.
FR-019 MUST: Scenario lab for budget reduction, member withdrawal, yield -20%, tariff/rate/O&M variation.
FR-020 SHOULD: Quote comparison, installer leads, financing referral with disclosure of referral incentives.
FR-021 SHOULD: O&M asset registry, CSV generation imports, expected-vs-actual comparison, maintenance tickets.
FR-022 SHOULD: Source-linked rule changes and scenario version history; user-readable audit trail.
FR-023 LATER: Authorised inverter API and DISCOM settlement integration, subject to contracts and approvals.
FR-024 LATER: Reviewed OCR/LLM bill extraction, never silently trusted for metering or policy facts.

4. BUSINESS RULES AND SAFETY INVARIANTS
BR-01 No transferable energy token, wallet, P2P trading or unlicensed resale.
BR-02 No automatic eligibility based on geography alone; consumer category, metering, project and policy version all required.
BR-03 No residential subsidy applied to C&I MSMEs by default.
BR-04 Official credits only sourced from authorised settlement; modelled credits remain labelled estimates.
BR-05 Individual meter usage cannot be inferred from inverter generation.
BR-06 A zero-participant solution is invalid when minimum participation is required.
BR-07 Missing/ambiguous readings block final reconciliation.
BR-08 No finance offer without verified provider and documented terms.

5. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
NFR-01 Security: HTTPS, secure password/session handling, least privilege, encryption at rest for sensitive data where supported.
NFR-02 Privacy: participant budget and bills private by default; explicit, revocable consent; data retention/deletion policy.
NFR-03 Integrity: immutable/versioned scenario inputs, rules, calculations, approval and settlement provenance.
NFR-04 Accuracy: deterministic calculations with unit tests; exact money rounding and share reconciliation.
NFR-05 Performance target (MVP): 20-member scenario solved within 10 seconds on demo hardware; benchmark and disclose solver timeouts.
NFR-06 Availability target (pilot proposal): 99.5% monthly, excluding planned maintenance; not a hackathon claim.
NFR-07 Accessibility: keyboard operability, labels, readable contrast, responsive mobile layouts.
NFR-08 Explainability: every amount shows input provenance, policy, assumptions and calculation version.
NFR-09 Maintainability: typed schemas, automated tests, migration scripts and configuration-driven policy records.
NFR-10 Portability: local SQLite demo; PostgreSQL production migration without changing core maths.

6. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
AC-01 Given 20 synthetic flats, three capacities (10/12/15 kW) produce valid feasible/infeasible reports.
AC-02 Reducing one member's budget reruns the solver and explains the change.
AC-03 Sum of allocation shares equals 100% within explicit rounding policy; sum of rupee allocations reconciles exactly.
AC-04 Attempted mixed-use sharing without verified pathway is blocked with reason.
AC-05 No output labelled 'official bill' unless it is a separately imported source document.
AC-06 Two different users cannot access each other's private bills or budgets.
AC-07 Solar credit already netted in utility bill cannot be subtracted again.
AC-08 Every subsidy and policy decision has a jurisdiction, source and effective date.

7. EXCLUSIONS
No actual electricity dispatch, energy trading, meter manipulation, DISCOM approval issuance, lending decisions, guarantees of savings, or automated official credit transfer.
